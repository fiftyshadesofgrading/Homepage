## The mystery
TK: two feature lines cross and suddenly there's a split point and an elevation you never set.

## What a site actually is
TK: a topology container; feature lines, parcels, grading groups and (optionally) alignments in the same site interact.

## The fixes
TK: one site per purpose, siteless alignments, moving objects between sites, "Site 1" as a trap.

## Where interaction is the point
TK: parcels built from alignments and lot lines; why some of it is a feature.
